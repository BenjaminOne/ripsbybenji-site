Delete your account

Last updated November 13, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Benji's Rips account, which is run by Benjamin Capital Partners, Inc., what we delete and what we keep. Our Privacy Policy has the full details.

Before you delete

Deleting your account is permanent. Any Cash and Rip credit left in your account and any Cards still in your Vault are forfeited and cannot be recovered. Cash out, and sell back or ship your Cards, before you delete. If a purchase, sell-back, cash-out or shipment is still in progress, the app asks you to wait until it is finished.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Benji's Rips app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Check your Cash, Rip credit, Cards, pending cash-outs and open shipments.
  4. Tap Forfeit balance and delete to confirm.

You are signed out and can no longer use the account straight away.

Delete your account without the app

Email support@ripsbybenji.com from the email address on your account, with "Delete my account" in the subject line. We will confirm it is your account before we delete it.

What we delete

We finish deleting your account within 30 days. This includes:

  • your sign-in, email address, username and state of residence;
  • your saved shipping address, spending limit and notification settings;
  • your app activity and analytics, and the device identifiers linked to your account;
  • any other personal information we no longer need.

Copies in our backups are deleted as the backups expire.

What we keep, and for how long

The law requires us to keep some records after your account is deleted, to meet anti-money-laundering, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention and legal obligations:

  • identity verification records;
  • records of your purchases, top-ups, sell-backs, shipments and cash-outs, and the account information linked to them.

We keep these for at least seven years after your last transaction, or longer where the law requires, and then delete them.
