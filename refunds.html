Refund Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · Last updated November 12, 2026 · Version 1.0

This Refund Policy is part of the Benji's Rips Terms of Use. It explains when Benjamin Capital Partners, Inc., doing business as Benji's Rips, gives refunds.

1. Purchases are final once a Pack is opened

A Pack purchase is final once the Pack is opened and its Card revealed. Funds you add as Rip credit are non-refundable and can only be spent on the Platform, except as set out below or where the law requires.

2. When we give refunds

  • Failed or duplicate charges: a purchase or top-up that failed but was charged, or was charged more than once.
  • Lost or damaged shipments: a shipment that is lost in transit or arrives damaged. We replace the Card or refund its value at the time of shipment, and refund the $17 shipping fee.
  • Authenticity or defect claims: a Card that is not authentic or is materially defective, reported within 90 days of receiving it. Phygitals supplies a reasonably equivalent Card or refunds the Pack price, which requires the return of the Card.
  • Our errors: a technical, pricing, inventory or processing error by us or our partners that affected your purchase.
  • Fraud: a charge you did not authorise, confirmed after our review.
  • Where the law requires a refund.

3. When we do not give refunds

  • Because you are unhappy with the Card you received or its value.
  • Because of the odds, the value range or the outcome of a Pack that matched what was disclosed.
  • Because market prices changed after the pull.
  • Because a Card was automatically sold back after you did not ship it or accept its sell-back offer within 7 days.
  • For Rip credit or Cash you have already spent.
  • For shipping fees once a shipment has been handed to the carrier, except for lost or damaged shipments.
  • Where your account was restricted for breaking the Terms, including for fraud, chargeback abuse or failed identity checks.

4. How refunds are paid

A refund is made to your original payment method or as Rip credit, at our choice unless the law requires otherwise. Refunds to a card can take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email hello@ripsbybenji.com within 30 days of the transaction with the date, the amount, the last four digits of the card and what went wrong. Report a missing, wrong or damaged shipment within 10 days of delivery. Never send a full card number. We reply within 5 business days.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank; we can usually fix the problem faster. If you file a chargeback that we find was not justified, we may reverse the related purchases, recover the amount from your balances and suspend your account under the Terms.
