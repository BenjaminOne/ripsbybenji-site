Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · Last updated November 13, 2026 · Version 1.1

This Privacy Policy explains how Benjamin Capital Partners, Inc., doing business as Benji's Rips ("we", "us" or "our"), collects, uses and shares personal information when you use the Benji's Rips app, the website at ripsbybenji.com and related services (the "Platform"), and the choices you have. It does not cover information collected by third parties, including sites we link to.

1. Age limits

The Platform is only for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we close the account and delete the information unless the law requires us to keep it. If you believe a minor is using the Platform, contact support@ripsbybenji.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us.

  • Identifiers and contact details: email address, state of residence and username, and your confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • Identity verification: name, address, date of birth and the last four digits of your Social Security number; where a check needs it, a government photo ID and a live selfie. These are collected and checked by our identity verification provider, Persona, and shared with our payment provider, Coinflow, for payouts.
  • Payment information: card or Apple Pay details are entered into Coinflow's secure form and processed by Coinflow. We receive a payment reference and limited card details, such as the card brand and last four digits, never the full card number.
  • Payout details: the bank account, debit card or PayPal account you choose for cash-outs, held by Coinflow.
  • Shipping information: the delivery name and address you give when you ship a Card.
  • Support communications: messages you send us and our replies.
  • Waitlist: if you joined our waitlist on our website, your email address, when you joined, any campaign tags on the link you used and the name of the website that sent you.

2.2 Information collected automatically.

  • Activity: Packs viewed and bought, Cards pulled, sell-backs, shipments, balances, top-ups, cash-outs, spending limits and in-app actions.
  • Device and connection: device model, operating system, app version, device identifiers, IP address, mobile carrier, time zone, language and screen size.
  • Location: your device's location when you buy, used to confirm you are in an eligible state. You must allow location access to buy Packs.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance data.
  • App integrity: signals from Apple and Google that help confirm requests come from a genuine copy of the app.
  • Payment fraud signals: when you top up, our payment provider Coinflow's fraud-prevention software collects details about your device and network and how you use the payment screens, to assess the risk of the payment.

2.3 Information from third parties. Results of identity, fraud and sanctions checks from Coinflow; payment and payout status from Coinflow; Card, sale and shipment status from Phygitals; and campaign attribution information from AppsFlyer, if you allow tracking.

3. How we use information

  • To create and run your account, and to provide Packs, Cards, sell-backs, shipments, balances and cash-outs.
  • To verify your age, identity and location, and to confirm you are in an eligible state.
  • To prevent fraud, chargebacks, multiple accounts, cheating and other abuse, and to keep the Platform secure.
  • To meet our legal duties, including anti-money-laundering, sanctions, tax and record-keeping obligations.
  • To apply your spending limit and send you reminders, such as before a sell-back offer ends.
  • To provide customer support and send account, transaction and legal messages.
  • To send push notifications and marketing messages, where you have allowed them.
  • To measure which campaigns bring people to the Platform, and to understand and improve how the Platform is used. Our app analytics are linked to your account, so we treat them as personal information.
  • For any other purpose we tell you about when we collect the information, or with your consent.

We use location only to confirm eligibility and help verify identity. We do not use automated decisions that produce legal effects about you, other than the eligibility, identity and fraud checks described above.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website uses only the cookies and storage needed for it to work, and loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. The app uses software development kits (SDKs) from the providers listed in Section 4. You can limit tracking in your device settings, including the iOS "Allow Apps to Request to Track" setting. We do not currently respond to Global Privacy Control or "Do Not Track" signals. To opt out of sharing, use the choices in Section 6.

6. Your choices and rights

6.1 Choices.

  • Push notifications, location and tracking: turn them off in your device settings. Without location, you cannot buy Packs.
  • Marketing: unsubscribe from emails using the link in each message.
  • Delete your account: in the app under Account, then Delete account, or as described at https://ripsbybenji.com/account-deletion. We disable access straight away and finish deleting within 30 days, except for records we must keep under Section 7. Copies in backups are deleted as the backups expire.

6.2 Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and how we use and share it;
  • get a copy of it in a portable format;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete it, subject to the exceptions in Section 7;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising;
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information (we use it only for identity verification, security and fraud prevention); and
  • not be treated differently for using these rights.

6.3 How to make a request. Email support@ripsbybenji.com or use the in-app support. We will verify your identity, usually by confirming details on your account, before acting. You may use an authorised agent, who must show us your written permission; we may still ask you to verify your identity. We respond within the time the law requires, normally 45 days.

6.4 Appeals. If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision or emailing support@ripsbybenji.com with "Appeal" in the subject line. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may contact your state Attorney General.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as your account is open and as long as needed for the purposes in this policy. After your account closes, we keep identity verification records, transaction records and related account information for at least seven years after your last transaction, or longer where the law requires, to meet anti-money-laundering, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention and legal obligations. We keep waitlist email addresses until you unsubscribe, ask us to delete them, or we close the waitlist after launch. Other information is deleted or de-identified when no longer needed.

8. Security

We protect personal information with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, logging and monitoring. Card details are handled by Coinflow and never stored on our systems. No system is perfectly secure. Keep your sign-in details and phone secure, and tell us at once if you think your account has been compromised.

9. Where information is processed

The Platform is offered only in the United States, and we process personal information in the United States. Some of our providers may process it in other countries under contracts that protect it.
