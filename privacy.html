Privacy notice

Website and waitlist · Last updated November 12, 2026

This notice explains what happens to the information you give us on ripsbybenji.com, including when you join the waitlist. The Benji's Rips app has not launched yet. It will come with its own Terms of Use and Privacy Policy, which you will see before you sign up in the app.

Who we are

Benji's Rips is a product of Benjamin Capital Partners, Inc. ("we", "us"), 1331 S Federal Highway, FL 33435, United States. You can reach us at hello@ripsbybenji.com.

What we collect when you join the waitlist

  • Your email address.
  • The date and time you joined.
  • If you came to us through a link with campaign tags (for example from an ad or a post), those tags: the source, medium, campaign and content names.
  • The name of the website that sent you to us, if your browser shares it. We keep only the site's name, not the full address of the page.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, date of birth or payment details on this website.

What we use it for

  • To email you when Benji's Rips launches, and with news about Benji's Rips before and around the launch.
  • To see which links and campaigns bring people to the waitlist, using the campaign tags and website name.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising pixels, analytics or tracking cookies. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address and browser details when the page loads.

Who handles your information for us

  • Google stores the waitlist for us in our company Google Workspace account, and serves the website's fonts.
  • GitHub hosts this website. Like any web host, its servers record technical details such as your IP address when you visit, to deliver the page and keep the service secure.
  • An email service provider may send our launch emails for us. It may only use your email address to send our messages.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, to protect our rights and users, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of our business.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address on the waitlist until you unsubscribe, ask us to delete it, or we close the waitlist after launch, whichever comes first.

Your choices

  • Every email we send has an unsubscribe link.
  • To see the information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email hello@ripsbybenji.com from the address you signed up with. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under your state's privacy law, and we will honor them as that law requires. You will not be treated differently for using them.

Adults only

Benji's Rips is for adults aged 18 or older in the United States. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has joined the waitlist, contact us and we will delete their information.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your email address, we will tell you by email first.
